## 📘 About the College

**Badri Narayan Mukteshwar College (BNMC)**, Barhiya, established with the vision of promoting higher education in the region, is affiliated with **Munger University**.  
The college offers various undergraduate programs across Arts, Science, and Commerce streams, focusing on academic excellence, moral values, and holistic development of students.

---

## 🎯 Mission & Vision

- To provide quality education to students from all sections of society.  
- To foster critical thinking, creativity, and moral responsibility.  
- To promote research, innovation, and social awareness.  
- To prepare students for careers in education, research, public service, and industry.

---

## 🌐 Website Overview

The BNMC website is developed using **HTML**, **CSS**, **Bootstrap**, and **JavaScript**, ensuring a responsive and user-friendly design.  
It serves as a digital platform for students, teachers, and visitors to access important information about the college.

### 🔸 Key Sections

- **Home:** College overview and announcements  
- **About Us:** History, mission, and vision of BNMC  
- **Departments:** Faculty and course information  
- **Gallery:** College campus and event photographs  
- **Admission:** Eligibility criteria and online admission process  
- **Contact:** Location, phone, and enquiry form
